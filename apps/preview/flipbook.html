The mini player

Play the film, then scroll on. While it plays, the player moves to the bottom-right corner as a small book, and its place here stays held, so nothing below it moves.

Above the small book, “Back to the player” scrolls the page back to it, and the close key puts it back in its place without pausing it. Escape closes it too. Scroll back up and it settles into its place on its own.

It doesn’t float before the film has played, in fullscreen, or while the film is in a picture-in-picture window.
