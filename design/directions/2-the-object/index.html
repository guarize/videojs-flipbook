Flipbook · a skin for Video.js 10

The first moving pictures were flipbooks. This player is one.

Fifty-two pages, six chapters, a fore-edge you can drag. I built it for the Video.js Skins Design Challenge, and the film inside is about where it comes from.

Fore-edge

The fore-edge is the timeline.

Each page’s edge carries a sliver of its own frame, so the closed book shows the film’s colours in order: page 1 on top, page 52 at the desk.

The six chapters are cut into it as a thumb index, like a dictionary’s letters. Drag along the edge and the pages riffle; put a thumb in a notch and the book opens there.

52pages

One sharp frame per page, a page every 1.23 seconds of film.

6notches

Gallop, Flight, Sprint, Leap, Spin, Rush. N turns to the next.

44px

Every notch, button and ribbon is at least that big to hit, and keys work without focusing the player.

Fore-edge, continued

Drag the edge, and the pages riffle.

Each page is one sharp frame, so riffling the book is watching the film by hand, as fast as your thumb moves. Let go and it stops on the page you’re on. Flick it and the pages keep turning on their own.
